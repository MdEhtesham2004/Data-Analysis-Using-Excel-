# Executive Summary
## Ferns and Petals – Sales Data Analysis

### 1. Project Overview

The Ferns and Petals Sales Analysis project focuses on analyzing sales data to understand business performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product revenue contribution and sales trends across different occasions, categories, locations and order timings.

The primary objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights using Microsoft Excel's data analytics capabilities. The analysis provides a consolidated view of key performance indicators (KPIs) and enables stakeholders to identify revenue patterns, evaluate product performance, understand customer spending behaviour and make informed business decisions.

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to present the findings through KPI cards, charts, PivotTables and slicers, allowing users to explore sales performance across different dimensions.

### 2. Business Objectives

The project aims to address the following business questions:

- What is the overall revenue generated by the business?
- How many orders have been placed, and what is the average customer spending?
- Which months contribute the highest revenue?
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which occasions drive the highest customer demand?
- Which product categories contribute significantly to overall sales?
- How is revenue distributed across different geographical locations?
- During which hours does the business experience higher sales activity?
- What opportunities exist to improve sales performance and customer engagement?

### 4. Key Business Insights

**A. Monthly Revenue Analysis**

The monthly revenue analysis highlights fluctuations in sales performance throughout the year.

- February and July demonstrate noticeable revenue peaks.
- October also records a significant increase in revenue.
- Revenue varies across different months, indicating potential seasonal purchasing patterns.
- Lower-performing months represent opportunities for targeted marketing campaigns and promotional activities.

**Business implication:** Understanding the factors behind high-performing months can help the business plan seasonal campaigns, manage inventory and improve revenue during relatively slower periods.

**B. Product Performance Analysis**

The top five products analyzed in the dashboard are:

- Desert Box
- Dolores Gift
- Harum Pack
- Magnam Set
- Quia Gift

Magnam Set appears to be the leading product by revenue among the five displayed products, followed by Quia Gift and Dolores Gift.

**Business implication:** Identifying high-revenue products helps the business prioritize inventory planning, promotional campaigns and product bundling strategies.

**C. Occasion-Based Revenue Analysis**

The occasion analysis compares revenue generated through different celebrations and gifting events.

- Anniversary appears to be the highest revenue-generating occasion.
- Raksha Bandhan and Holi also demonstrate strong revenue contributions.
- Birthday and Valentine's Day contribute comparatively lower revenue.
- Diwali shows a smaller contribution in the displayed chart.

**Business implication:** Occasion-based purchasing represents an important business opportunity. Marketing campaigns, personalized product recommendations and promotional offers can be aligned with occasions that generate stronger customer demand.

**D. Product Category Analysis**

The category analysis evaluates revenue contribution across different product categories.

- Colors appears to be the leading category.
- Soft Toys and Sweets are also significant contributors.
- Cakes, Plants and Mugs demonstrate comparatively lower revenue contributions.

**Business implication:** The business can prioritize high-performing categories while exploring cross-selling, discounts and bundled offers to improve the performance of lower-contributing categories.

**E. Geographical Sales Analysis**

The location analysis presents the geographical distribution of orders and revenue across multiple locations, including Dibrugarh, Bidhannagar, Bilaspur, Dhanbad, Guntakal and Haridwar.

The visualization helps stakeholders understand how sales are distributed across the locations included in the dataset.

**Business implication:** Further comparison of location-level revenue can help identify high-performing markets, understand regional demand and plan location-specific marketing activities.

**F. Hourly Revenue Analysis**

The hourly revenue analysis examines sales performance across different order hours.

- Revenue fluctuates throughout the day.
- Certain hours demonstrate noticeable revenue peaks.
- Other hours show comparatively lower sales activity.

**Business implication:** Understanding hourly purchasing patterns can help the business optimize promotional timing, customer engagement activities and operational planning.

### 5. Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard findings, the following recommendations can help improve business performance:

1. **Seasonal Marketing:** Analyze high-performing months and replicate successful promotional strategies during lower-revenue periods.

2. **Product Optimization:** Prioritize high-revenue products through targeted promotions, inventory planning and product bundles.

3. **Occasion-Based Campaigns:** Develop personalized marketing campaigns around anniversaries, Raksha Bandhan and Holi to capitalize on observed demand.

4. **Category-Level Growth:** Improve sales of lower-contributing categories through discounts, cross-selling and attractive product combinations.

5. **Regional Marketing:** Use location-level sales comparisons to identify promising markets and develop region-specific promotional strategies.

6. **Delivery Optimization:** Evaluate delivery operations to identify opportunities to reduce the reported average delivery time of 5.53 days and improve customer satisfaction.

7. **Customer Engagement:** Use average customer spending and purchasing patterns to develop customer retention initiatives and personalized offers.

### 6. Tools and Technologies Used

The project uses Microsoft Excel and its data analytics capabilities:

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data analysis, calculations and dashboard development.
- **Power Query:** Data extraction, cleaning, transformation and preparation.
- **Power Pivot:** Data modeling and relationship management.
- **DAX:** Creating measures and calculating business KPIs.
- **PivotTables:** Summarizing and analyzing sales data across different dimensions.
- **PivotCharts:** Visualizing revenue and sales performance.
- **Slicers and Timelines:** Enabling interactive filtering and dashboard exploration.

### 7. Project Outcome

The project demonstrates how raw sales data can be transformed into an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

Through the analysis, the business gains visibility into overall revenue, order volume, customer spending, product performance, occasion-based demand, category contribution, geographical distribution and hourly sales trends.

The dashboard provides stakeholders with a consolidated view of sales performance and supports data-driven decision-making in areas such as marketing, product strategy, inventory planning, customer engagement and operational efficiency.

### 8. Conclusion

The Ferns and Petals Sales Analysis project demonstrates the practical application of Excel-based data analytics and business intelligence techniques to a sales dataset.

The analysis identifies important revenue patterns, highlights products and occasions contributing to sales, and provides opportunities for improving business performance. By combining data preparation, data modeling, DAX calculations, PivotTables and interactive visualizations, the project converts raw transactional data into actionable business insights.

Ultimately, the dashboard serves as a decision-support tool that enables stakeholders to monitor performance, identify business opportunities and develop strategies based on sales
