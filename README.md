## Sales Insights Summary

- Highest Sales Month: March recorded the highest sales, indicating strong customer demand during this period.
- Women as the Primary Customers: Women contributed 69% of total sales, making them the key target audience.
- Order Delivery Performance: Approximately 92% of orders were successfully delivered, reflecting good delivery performance.
- Top 5 Performing States: Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Telangana, and Tamil Nadu contributed significantly to sales.
- Top Customer Category: Adult women contributed 34.59% of total sales, making them an important segment to target.
- Top Sales Channels: Amazon, Flipkart, and Ajio (assuming “AM Aleazon” refers to Ajio) are the key channels to focus on for improving sales.

## Recommended Business Strategy

1. Target adult women: Focus marketing campaigns and product promotions on adult women, who represent a major sales segment.
2. Offer discounts and deals: Introduce special offers, seasonal discounts, and combo deals to attract more female customers and increase purchase frequency.
3. Prioritize major sales channels: Strengthen product visibility, advertising, and promotional campaigns on Amazon, Flipkart, and Ajio.
4. Focus on top-performing states: Allocate more marketing resources to Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Telangana, and Tamil Nadu.
5. Improve delivery performance: Work toward increasing the order delivery rate beyond 92% to improve customer satisfaction.

### Overall Conclusion

The business should focus on adult women as its primary target audience, using attractive discounts and promotional campaigns across Amazon, Flipkart, and Ajio. By concentrating on the top-performing states and preparing targeted campaigns around high-sales periods such as March, the business can improve sales, customer engagement, and overall growth.
