# Executive Summary
## Ferns and Petals – Sales Data Analysis

### 1. Project Overview

The Ferns and Petals Sales Analysis project focuses on analyzing sales data to understand business performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product revenue contribution and sales trends across different occasions, categories, locations and order timings.

The primary objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights using Microsoft Excel's data analytics capabilities. The analysis provides a consolidated view of key performance indicators (KPIs) and enables stakeholders to identify revenue patterns, evaluate product performance, understand customer spending behaviour and make informed business decisions.

An interactive Excel dashboard was developed to present the findings through KPI cards, charts, PivotTables and slicers, allowing users to explore sales performance across different dimensions.

### 2. Business Objectives

The project aims to address the following business questions:

- What is the overall revenue generated by the business?
- How many orders have been placed, and what is the average customer spending?
- Which months contribute the highest revenue?
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- Which occasions drive the highest customer demand?
- Which product categories contribute significantly to overall sales?
- How is revenue distributed across different geographical locations?
- During which hours does the business experience higher sales activity?
- What opportunities exist to improve sales performance and customer engagement?

### 3. Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard highlights the following business metrics:

| KPI | Reported Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | ₹35,20,984 |
| Total Orders Placed | 1,000 |
| Average Customer Spending | ₹3,520.98 |
| Average Delivery Days | 5.53 days |
| Total Orders Revenue | ₹5,00,500 |

These KPIs provide an overview of the business's financial performance, order volume, customer spending and delivery operations.

### 4. Key Business Insights

**A. Monthly Revenue Analysis**

The monthly revenue analysis highlights fluctuations in sales performance throughout the year.

- February and July demonstrate noticeable revenue peaks.
- October also records a significant increase in revenue.
- Revenue varies across different months, indicating potential seasonal purchasing patterns.
- Lower-performing months represent opportunities for targeted marketing campaigns and promotional activities.

**Business implication:** Understanding the factors behind high-performing months can help the business plan seasonal campaigns, manage inventory and improve revenue during relatively slower periods.

**B. Product Performance Analysis**

The top five products analyzed in the dashboard are:

- Desert Box
- Dolores Gift
- Harum Pack
- Magnam Set
- Quia Gift

Magnam Set appears to be the leading product by revenue among the five displayed products, followed by Quia Gift and Dolores Gift.

**Business implication:** Identifying high-revenue products helps the business prioritize inventory planning, promotional campaigns and product bundling strategies.

**C. Occasion-Based Revenue Analysis**

The occasion analysis compares revenue generated through different celebrations and gifting events.

- Anniversary appears to be the highest revenue-generating occasion.
- Raksha Bandhan and Holi also demonstrate strong revenue contributions.
- Birthday and Valentine's Day contribute comparatively lower revenue.
- Diwali shows a smaller contribution in the displayed chart.

**Business implication:** Occasion-based purchasing represents an important business opportunity. Marketing campaigns, personalized product recommendations and promotional offers can be aligned with occasions that generate stronger customer demand.

**D. Product Category Analysis**

The category analysis evaluates revenue contribution across different product categories.

- Colors appears to be the leading category.
- Soft Toys and Sweets are also significant contributors.
- Cakes, Plants and Mugs demonstrate comparatively lower revenue contributions.

**Business implication:** The business can prioritize high-performing categories while exploring cross-selling, discounts and bundled offers to improve the performance of lower-contributing categories.

**E. Geographical Sales Analysis**

The location analysis presents the geographical distribution of orders and revenue across multiple locations, including Dibrugarh, Bidhannagar, Bilaspur, Dhanbad, Guntakal and Haridwar.

The visualization helps stakeholders understand how sales are distributed across the locations included in the dataset.

**Business implication:** Further comparison of location-level revenue can help identify high-performing markets, understand regional demand and plan location-specific marketing activities.

**F. Hourly Revenue Analysis**

The hourly revenue analysis examines sales performance across different order hours.

- Revenue fluctuates throughout the day.
- Certain hours demonstrate noticeable revenue peaks.
- Other hours show comparatively lower sales activity.

**Business implication:** Understanding hourly purchasing patterns can help the business optimize promotional timing, customer engagement activities and operational planning.

### 5. Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard findings, the following recommendations can help improve business performance:

1. **Seasonal Marketing:** Analyze high-performing months and replicate successful promotional strategies during lower-revenue periods.

2. **Product Optimization:** Prioritize high-revenue products through targeted promotions, inventory planning and product bundles.

3. **Occasion-Based Campaigns:** Develop personalized marketing campaigns around anniversaries, Raksha Bandhan and Holi to capitalize on observed demand.

4. **Category-Level Growth:** Improve sales of lower-contributing categories through discounts, cross-selling and attractive product combinations.

5. **Regional Marketing:** Use location-level sales comparisons to identify promising markets and develop region-specific promotional strategies.

6. **Delivery Optimization:** Evaluate delivery operations to identify opportunities to reduce the reported average delivery time of 5.53 days and improve customer satisfaction.

7. **Customer Engagement:** Use average customer spending and purchasing patterns to develop customer retention initiatives and personalized offers.

### 6. Tools and Technologies Used

The project uses Microsoft Excel and its data analytics capabilities:

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data analysis, calculations and dashboard development.
- **Power Query:** Data extraction, cleaning, transformation and preparation.
- **Power Pivot:** Data modeling and relationship management.
- **DAX:** Creating measures and calculating business KPIs.
- **PivotTables:** Summarizing and analyzing sales data across different dimensions.
- **PivotCharts:** Visualizing revenue and sales performance.
- **Slicers and Timelines:** Enabling interactive filtering and dashboard exploration.

### 7. Project Outcome

The project demonstrates how raw sales data can be transformed into an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

Through the analysis, the business gains visibility into overall revenue, order volume, customer spending, product performance, occasion-based demand, category contribution, geographical distribution and hourly sales trends.

The dashboard provides stakeholders with a consolidated view of sales performance and supports data-driven decision-making in areas such as marketing, product strategy, inventory planning, customer engagement and operational efficiency.

### 8. Conclusion

The Ferns and Petals Sales Analysis project demonstrates the practical application of Excel-based data analytics and business intelligence techniques to a sales dataset.

The analysis identifies important revenue patterns, highlights products and occasions contributing to sales, and provides opportunities for improving business performance. By combining data preparation, data modeling, DAX calculations, PivotTables and interactive visualizations, the project converts raw transactional data into actionable business insights.

Ultimately, the dashboard serves as a decision-support tool that enables stakeholders to monitor performance, identify business opportunities and develop strategies based on sales

# Executive Summary
## Placement Drive – Student Interview & Recruitment Analytics

### 1. Project Overview

The Placement Drive – Student Interview & Recruitment Analytics project is a real-world data collection, candidate evaluation and business intelligence project developed to analyze student performance during a technical placement interview process.

Unlike projects built using synthetic or sample datasets, this project is based on actual applicant information and interview evaluation records collected during a student placement drive.

I was directly involved in the interview process, where I conducted technical interviews, assessed candidates across multiple skill areas, documented individual performance, recorded interviewer feedback and maintained the evaluation records.

The collected information was organized and analyzed using Microsoft Excel to transform individual interview assessments into structured data and meaningful insights. An interactive dashboard was developed to provide a consolidated view of applicant participation, candidate performance, skill-wise assessment, selection grouping and overall interview outcomes.

The project demonstrates the practical application of data analytics and business intelligence in recruitment operations, particularly in evaluating technical readiness, identifying skill gaps and supporting structured candidate assessment.

### 2. Project Objectives

The primary objective was to establish a structured, data-driven approach to analyzing student interview performance and understanding candidate readiness for placement opportunities.

The key objectives included:

- Collecting and organizing applicant information for the placement drive.
- Tracking interview attendance and participation.
- Conducting technical interviews and evaluating candidates across predefined assessment categories.
- Recording individual candidate scores and qualitative interviewer feedback.
- Establishing evaluation criteria to assess technical and professional competencies.
- Analyzing candidate performance across different skill dimensions.
- Categorizing candidates based on their overall assessment scores.
- Identifying top-performing candidates and areas requiring improvement.
- Developing an interactive Excel dashboard to communicate interview outcomes and assessment insights.

### 3. Real-World Data Collection and Interview Process

The foundation of this project is primary data collected during an actual student placement drive.

The process involved managing applicant information and conducting interviews rather than relying on an externally provided or randomly generated dataset.

The data collection and evaluation process included:

**Applicant Data Collection**

Collected and maintained applicant details, including:
- Candidate information and educational background.
- Degree, academic stream and year of passing.
- Academic performance information.
- Interview attendance status.

**Interview Execution**

Personally conducted technical interviews and assessed students based on their understanding of programming, data science concepts, analytical thinking and project experience.

**Interview Evaluation**

Recorded candidate performance and feedback across different assessment areas, including:
- Statistics and Machine Learning Fundamentals.
- Python and SQL Skills.
- Project Understanding.
- Problem Solving and Logical Thinking.
- Communication and Attitude.

**Feedback Documentation**

Maintained qualitative observations about candidates' technical knowledge, communication, confidence, conceptual understanding and areas requiring improvement.

This created a structured dataset that could be analyzed to understand both individual candidate performance and overall interview trends.

### 4. Interview Evaluation Framework

A predefined evaluation framework was used to assess candidates across five major competency areas.

| Evaluation Category | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Statistics and ML Fundamentals | 30% |
| Python / SQL Skills | 20% |
| Project Understanding | 25% |
| Problem Solving and Logical Thinking | 15% |
| Communication and Attitude | 10% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

Each competency was evaluated using a rating scale of 1 to 5.

The evaluation framework also established a target average score of 4.2 out of 5 as the reference benchmark.

This approach provided a consistent structure for comparing candidates across technical and professional competencies.

### 5. Data Preparation and Analysis

After completing the interview evaluations, the collected records were organized and prepared for analysis using Microsoft Excel.

The analysis involved:

- Consolidating applicant and interview records.
- Organizing candidate information and assessment scores.
- Maintaining interview attendance and evaluation status.
- Structuring technical and HR feedback.
- Calculating candidate-level average scores.
- Comparing skill-wise performance across candidates.
- Creating PivotTables to summarize evaluation results.
- Preparing analytical tables for candidate grouping and ranking.

The prepared data was then used to develop multiple analytical views, enabling the interview results to be examined from different perspectives.

### 6. Dashboard KPIs and Key Findings

The Dashboard v2 provides an overview of the actual interview process and candidate evaluation outcomes.

**Applicant Participation**

- Total Applicants: 64
- Candidates Attended: 29
- Interview Attendance Rate: 45.31%
- Candidates Scored: 23

The attendance analysis shows that 29 out of 64 applicants participated in the interview process. Of these, 23 candidates had recorded scores in the evaluation dataset.

**Candidate Performance**

- Average Candidate Score: 2.67 out of 5
- Candidates on Hold: 8
- Candidates Not Ready: 15

Among the 23 scored candidates, approximately 34.78% were categorized as Hold and 65.22% as Not Ready.

The analysis highlights an opportunity to improve candidate preparedness, particularly in technical fundamentals, practical programming and interview communication.

**Skill-Wise Performance Analysis**

The average scores across the five assessment categories were:

| Assessment Category | Average Score |
|---|---:|
| Statistics and ML Fundamentals | 2.15 |
| Python / SQL Skills | 2.16 |
| Project Understanding | 2.10 |
| Problem Solving | 2.10 |
| Communication and Attitude | 2.16 |

The scores indicate that performance was relatively consistent across all five assessment areas, with averages ranging from 2.10 to 2.16.

Python / SQL Skills and Communication and Attitude recorded the highest category averages at 2.16, while Project Understanding and Problem Solving recorded 2.10.

However, all category averages remained below the target benchmark of 4.2, indicating considerable scope for improvement across the assessed competencies.

**Candidate Ranking and Performance Comparison**

The dashboard also includes a candidate performance heatmap and top-performer analysis.

These visualizations help compare individual candidates across the five evaluation categories, identify stronger competency areas and highlight candidates who performed relatively well during the interview process.

**Gender-Based Analysis**

The dashboard includes a gender-wise distribution of candidates across the Hold and Not Ready groups.

This provides an additional demographic view of the assessment results. These figures should be interpreted descriptively and not as evidence of differences in capability or selection fairness.

### 7. Key Business and Operational Insights

The analysis provides several insights that can support future placement activities and student preparation initiatives.

**1. Applicant Engagement**

The 45.31% interview attendance rate indicates that less than half of the registered applicants attended the interview. Improving candidate communication, scheduling and interview engagement may help increase participation in future drives.

**2. Technical Skill Development**

The relatively low average scores across Statistics and ML, Python / SQL, Project Understanding and Problem Solving suggest a need for stronger practical and conceptual preparation.

**3. Project Explanation and Practical Knowledge**

Project understanding received an average score of 2.10 out of 5. This highlights an opportunity to help students develop the ability to explain their projects, technical decisions, implementation approaches and practical contributions.

**4. Communication and Interview Readiness**

Communication and Attitude recorded an average of 2.16 out of 5. Students can benefit from additional practice in explaining technical concepts, structuring answers, demonstrating confidence and communicating their thought processes.

**5. Structured Candidate Assessment**

Using a predefined evaluation framework made it possible to compare candidates across consistent assessment categories rather than relying only on subjective overall impressions.

**6. Identification of Training Requirements**

The skill-wise assessment provides a foundation for identifying training priorities and designing focused improvement programs before subsequent placement opportunities.

### 8. Recommended Actions

Based on the findings, the following actions can be considered:

- Organize focused revision sessions covering Statistics, Machine Learning, Python and SQL fundamentals.
- Introduce practical coding and problem-solving exercises.
- Conduct project explanation and technical presentation sessions.
- Provide mock technical interviews and communication practice.
- Develop individual improvement plans based on recorded interviewer feedback.
- Track candidate progress through periodic assessments.
- Improve applicant communication and interview scheduling to increase attendance.
- Repeat the evaluation process in future placement drives to compare improvements over time.

**Microsoft Excel**
- Data collection and management.
- Data cleaning and preparation.
- Formula-based calculations.

**Excel Analytical Features**

- Category-wise and candidate-wise performance analysis.
- Visual reporting and data summarization.

### 10. Project Outcome and Deliverables

The project resulted in a structured interview evaluation dataset and an interactive placement analytics dashboard.

The major deliverables include:

- Consolidated applicant database.
- Interview attendance tracking.
- Candidate assessment and scoring records.
- Technical and HR interviewer feedback.
- Weighted interview evaluation criteria.
- Candidate performance comparisons.
- Skill-wise assessment analysis.
- Candidate grouping and ranking.
- Interactive Excel dashboard.

The dashboard transforms individual interview records into a consolidated analytical view, helping stakeholders understand applicant participation, candidate readiness and competency-level performance.

### 11. Conclusion

The Placement Drive – Student Interview & Recruitment Analytics project demonstrates how real-world interview data can be collected, structured and transformed into actionable insights using Microsoft Excel.

My involvement extended beyond dashboard development. I participated directly in the recruitment assessment process by collecting applicant information, conducting technical interviews, evaluating student competencies, documenting feedback, maintaining the data and developing the final analytical dashboard.

This project combines practical interviewing experience, structured candidate assessment, data preparation, analytical thinking and business intelligence.

It demonstrates the use of data analytics not only for understanding business performance but also for solving real operational problems in recruitment, student assessment and placement readiness.

Ultimately, the project provides a data-driven foundation for evaluating student competencies, identifying skill gaps and planning targeted training interventions to improve future placement outcomes.
