# Executive Summary
## Placement Drive – Student Interview & Recruitment Analytics

### 1. Project Overview

The Placement Drive – Student Interview & Recruitment Analytics project is a real-world data collection, candidate evaluation and business intelligence project developed to analyze student performance during a technical placement interview process.

Unlike projects built using synthetic or sample datasets, this project is based on actual applicant information and interview evaluation records collected during a student placement drive.

I was directly involved in the interview process, where I conducted technical interviews, assessed candidates across multiple skill areas, documented individual performance, recorded interviewer feedback and maintained the evaluation records.

The collected information was organized and analyzed using Microsoft Excel to transform individual interview assessments into structured data and meaningful insights. An interactive dashboard was developed to provide a consolidated view of applicant participation, candidate performance, skill-wise assessment, selection grouping and overall interview outcomes.

The project demonstrates the practical application of data analytics and business intelligence in recruitment operations, particularly in evaluating technical readiness, identifying skill gaps and supporting structured candidate assessment.

### 2. Project Objectives

The primary objective was to establish a structured, data-driven approach to analyzing student interview performance and understanding candidate readiness for placement opportunities.

The key objectives included:

- Collecting and organizing applicant information for the placement drive.
- Tracking interview attendance and participation.
- Conducting technical interviews and evaluating candidates across predefined assessment categories.
- Recording individual candidate scores and qualitative interviewer feedback.
- Establishing evaluation criteria to assess technical and professional competencies.
- Analyzing candidate performance across different skill dimensions.
- Categorizing candidates based on their overall assessment scores.
- Identifying top-performing candidates and areas requiring improvement.
- Developing an interactive Excel dashboard to communicate interview outcomes and assessment insights.

### 3. Real-World Data Collection and Interview Process

The foundation of this project is primary data collected during an actual student placement drive.

The process involved managing applicant information and conducting interviews rather than relying on an externally provided or randomly generated dataset.

The data collection and evaluation process included:

**Applicant Data Collection**

Collected and maintained applicant details, including:
- Candidate information and educational background.
- Degree, academic stream and year of passing.
- Academic performance information.
- Interview attendance status.

**Interview Execution**

Personally conducted technical interviews and assessed students based on their understanding of programming, data science concepts, analytical thinking and project experience.

**Interview Evaluation**

Recorded candidate performance and feedback across different assessment areas, including:
- Statistics and Machine Learning Fundamentals.
- Python and SQL Skills.
- Project Understanding.
- Problem Solving and Logical Thinking.
- Communication and Attitude.

**Feedback Documentation**

Maintained qualitative observations about candidates' technical knowledge, communication, confidence, conceptual understanding and areas requiring improvement.

This created a structured dataset that could be analyzed to understand both individual candidate performance and overall interview trends.

### 4. Interview Evaluation Framework

A predefined evaluation framework was used to assess candidates across five major competency areas.

| Evaluation Category | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Statistics and ML Fundamentals | 30% |
| Python / SQL Skills | 20% |
| Project Understanding | 25% |
| Problem Solving and Logical Thinking | 15% |
| Communication and Attitude | 10% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

Each competency was evaluated using a rating scale of 1 to 5.

The evaluation framework also established a target average score of 4.2 out of 5 as the reference benchmark.

This approach provided a consistent structure for comparing candidates across technical and professional competencies.

### 5. Data Preparation and Analysis

After completing the interview evaluations, the collected records were organized and prepared for analysis using Microsoft Excel.

The analysis involved:

- Consolidating applicant and interview records.
- Organizing candidate information and assessment scores.
- Maintaining interview attendance and evaluation status.
- Structuring technical and HR feedback.
- Calculating candidate-level average scores.
- Comparing skill-wise performance across candidates.
- Creating PivotTables to summarize evaluation results.
- Preparing analytical tables for candidate grouping and ranking.

The prepared data was then used to develop multiple analytical views, enabling the interview results to be examined from different perspectives.

### 6. Dashboard KPIs and Key Findings

The Dashboard v2 provides an overview of the actual interview process and candidate evaluation outcomes.

**Applicant Participation**

- Total Applicants: 64
- Candidates Attended: 29
- Interview Attendance Rate: 45.31%
- Candidates Scored: 23

The attendance analysis shows that 29 out of 64 applicants participated in the interview process. Of these, 23 candidates had recorded scores in the evaluation dataset.

**Candidate Performance**

- Average Candidate Score: 2.67 out of 5
- Candidates on Hold: 8
- Candidates Not Ready: 15

Among the 23 scored candidates, approximately 34.78% were categorized as Hold and 65.22% as Not Ready.

The analysis highlights an opportunity to improve candidate preparedness, particularly in technical fundamentals, practical programming and interview communication.

**Skill-Wise Performance Analysis**

The average scores across the five assessment categories were:

| Assessment Category | Average Score |
|---|---:|
| Statistics and ML Fundamentals | 2.15 |
| Python / SQL Skills | 2.16 |
| Project Understanding | 2.10 |
| Problem Solving | 2.10 |
| Communication and Attitude | 2.16 |

The scores indicate that performance was relatively consistent across all five assessment areas, with averages ranging from 2.10 to 2.16.

Python / SQL Skills and Communication and Attitude recorded the highest category averages at 2.16, while Project Understanding and Problem Solving recorded 2.10.

However, all category averages remained below the target benchmark of 4.2, indicating considerable scope for improvement across the assessed competencies.

**Candidate Ranking and Performance Comparison**

The dashboard also includes a candidate performance heatmap and top-performer analysis.

These visualizations help compare individual candidates across the five evaluation categories, identify stronger competency areas and highlight candidates who performed relatively well during the interview process.

**Gender-Based Analysis**

The dashboard includes a gender-wise distribution of candidates across the Hold and Not Ready groups.

This provides an additional demographic view of the assessment results. These figures should be interpreted descriptively and not as evidence of differences in capability or selection fairness.

### 7. Key Business and Operational Insights

The analysis provides several insights that can support future placement activities and student preparation initiatives.

**1. Applicant Engagement**

The 45.31% interview attendance rate indicates that less than half of the registered applicants attended the interview. Improving candidate communication, scheduling and interview engagement may help increase participation in future drives.

**2. Technical Skill Development**

The relatively low average scores across Statistics and ML, Python / SQL, Project Understanding and Problem Solving suggest a need for stronger practical and conceptual preparation.

**3. Project Explanation and Practical Knowledge**

Project understanding received an average score of 2.10 out of 5. This highlights an opportunity to help students develop the ability to explain their projects, technical decisions, implementation approaches and practical contributions.

**4. Communication and Interview Readiness**

Communication and Attitude recorded an average of 2.16 out of 5. Students can benefit from additional practice in explaining technical concepts, structuring answers, demonstrating confidence and communicating their thought processes.

**5. Structured Candidate Assessment**

Using a predefined evaluation framework made it possible to compare candidates across consistent assessment categories rather than relying only on subjective overall impressions.

**6. Identification of Training Requirements**

The skill-wise assessment provides a foundation for identifying training priorities and designing focused improvement programs before subsequent placement opportunities.

### 8. Recommended Actions

Based on the findings, the following actions can be considered:

- Organize focused revision sessions covering Statistics, Machine Learning, Python and SQL fundamentals.
- Introduce practical coding and problem-solving exercises.
- Conduct project explanation and technical presentation sessions.
- Provide mock technical interviews and communication practice.
- Develop individual improvement plans based on recorded interviewer feedback.
- Track candidate progress through periodic assessments.
- Improve applicant communication and interview scheduling to increase attendance.
- Repeat the evaluation process in future placement drives to compare improvements over time.
